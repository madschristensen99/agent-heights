Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2025

Overview

Agent Heights ("we", "our", or "the Service") is an AI agent platform that lets users hire and manage AI agents connected to external services via Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication and account management)
  • Display name and profile preferences

Agent and Usage Data

  • Agent configurations, prompts, and conversation history
  • API usage records for billing and rate limiting
  • WebSocket session data for real-time agent communication

OAuth Tokens (Google Workspace and other MCP integrations)

  • When you connect an external service (e.g., Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive), we store OAuth access and refresh tokens encrypted in our database
  • These tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests to the connected service on your behalf
  • We never share your OAuth tokens with third parties
  • You can revoke access at any time from your account settings or the provider's security settings

Payment Information

  • Subscription and payment processing is handled by Stripe — we do not store your full credit card number
  • We store transaction records and billing history for account management

How Google Workspace MCP Servers Access Your Data

When you connect a Google Workspace agent (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, Google Calendar, Google Chat, or Google Contacts), the agent accesses your Google data through Google's official MCP servers using OAuth 2.0. The specific data accessed depends on the scopes you authorize:

  • Gmail: Read email threads and create drafts (does not send emails automatically)
  • Google Drive: Search, read, and manage files you've opened or created through the app
  • Google Docs: Read and update documents
  • Google Sheets: Read and update spreadsheet data and formulas
  • Google Slides: Read and update presentations
  • Google Calendar: List, search, create, update, and delete events
  • Google Chat: Read and send messages in spaces the bot is added to
  • Google Contacts: Read your contacts, profile, and directory (read-only)

All data access is performed through Google's official MCP servers. We do not store the content of your Google Workspace data — only the OAuth tokens needed to make authenticated requests.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To authenticate you and manage your account
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To communicate with you about your account and the Service
  • To monitor and improve Service performance and reliability
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security policies
  • OAuth tokens and API keys are encrypted using AES-256-GCM
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted to authenticated users only

Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • You can request account deletion at any time — data is permanently removed within 30 days
  • OAuth tokens are removed when you disconnect a service or delete your account
  • Billing records may be retained for legal compliance purposes

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Revoke OAuth access for any connected service
  • Export your agent configurations and conversation history
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party providers:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database hosting
  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • Google: Workspace MCP integrations (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat, People)
  • Resend: Transactional email delivery
  • Railway: Application hosting

Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.
